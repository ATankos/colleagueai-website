Displayed ranges are indicative starting points, not binding quotations. Final pricing depends on the selected use case, implementation scope, system integrations, data readiness, governance requirements, deployment model, support requirements and customization.

Why Tier 4 carries a higher range

Risk and governance agents are not more valuable by default; they are more demanding to build and evidence. The range reflects additional work on control design, auditability, testing, human oversight, evidence capture, escalation workflows, policy alignment and regulatory or internal-governance requirements.

Enterprise multi-agent systems

Tier 5 applies where several specialized agents operate together across functions and systems. Scope typically adds centralized reporting, advanced security and access requirements, shared governance controls, multi-stage approvals and wider organizational rollout. These solutions are designed and priced individually.

Scoping

What determines your final investment?

Six factors move an engagement within, or outside, its indicative range. They are assessed during discovery and confirmed in the proposal.

Workflow complexity

Number of process steps, exceptions, decisions and business rules.

Agent autonomy

Whether the agent drafts, recommends, executes actions or coordinates other agents.

Integrations

Connections to enterprise systems, APIs, databases, document repositories and communication platforms.

Governance requirements

Audit trails, approvals, human oversight, controls, evidence logging and policy alignment.

Data readiness

Availability, accessibility, structure, sensitivity and quality of the required business data.

Implementation scope

Testing, localization, documentation, user training, deployment support and post-launch optimization.

Questions, answered

Pricing FAQ

Is the displayed price a one-time fee?

The commercial structure depends on the solution. An engagement may include a one-time agent package, implementation services and an ongoing governance, support or update component.

Why do agents have different prices?

Pricing reflects differences in workflow complexity, autonomy, integrations, business criticality, governance requirements and implementation effort.

Can we start with one agent?

Yes. Organizations can begin with a focused use case and expand into a wider portfolio or multi-agent environment after validating the initial business outcome.

Are integrations included?

Standard integrations may be included in the proposed scope. Complex, custom or legacy-system integrations are assessed separately during discovery.

Can the solution run in our environment?

Deployment options depend on the selected technology architecture, security requirements and customer environment. Available models should be confirmed during solution design.

Do you offer multi-agent solutions?

Yes. Multi-agent solutions are designed and priced individually based on the number of agents, workflows, integrations, governance controls and organizational scope.

Do you guarantee ROI?

Expected benefits and success measures can be defined during discovery. Actual results depend on adoption, process quality, data readiness, implementation scope and organizational execution.

Can pricing change after discovery?

Yes. The initial range is indicative. A final proposal is prepared after the required scope, dependencies, risks and delivery responsibilities have been confirmed.
