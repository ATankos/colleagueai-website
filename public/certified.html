The programme

Continuous Certification

When you buy an agent package, the licence is one-time and perpetual: it is yours whether or not you ever pay us again. Continuous Certification is the separate, optional subscription that keeps the version you bought listed as a current Colleague AI Certified Release — and gives you the updated certified release if a change inside the covered scope means the standard package has to change.

What this is not. The Colleague AI Certified standard is Colleague AI’s own programme. It is not accreditation, attestation or certification by any third party, notified body or regulator, and it is not legal, regulatory or compliance advice. We do not represent that your use of an agent complies with any law that applies to you: that depends on your configuration, your data, your processes, your sector and your jurisdiction — none of which we can see.

What the subscription includes

  • Active certification status. Your purchased version is listed as a current Colleague AI Certified Release for as long as the subscription runs.
  • Monitoring of the covered scope. We watch for changes inside the scope below that would affect our own certification requirements.
  • Regulatory-driven updates. If a change inside the covered scope requires a change to the standard agent package, you get the resulting certified release at no further licence fee.
  • A verifiable certificate. A Certificate ID your auditor, client or board can check without going through you.

What it does not include

  • Support, implementation or customisation work
  • Any processing of your business data
  • A feature roadmap, or releases on a schedule

Updates are conditional, not periodic. If nothing inside the covered scope changes in a given year, no new release is produced and none is owed. We would rather say that plainly than let “updates” be read as an annual version you are paying for.

Covered scope

The update obligation is limited to the following, as they affect the standard agent package we ship:

  • The EU AI Act and its amending acts, in respect of the classification, human-oversight, logging and transparency characteristics of the package
  • ISO/IEC 42001 control expectations, as reflected in the Colleague AI Certified standard
  • Revisions to the Colleague AI Certified standard itself
  • Microsoft Copilot Studio and Power Platform changes that break a shipped connect package

Your own sector rules — DORA, MiFID, HIPAA, national implementations and anything specific to your industry or licence — are outside the covered scope and are not part of the update obligation.

Certification applies to the version as supplied

Certification covers the licensed version as delivered, and configuration within its documented supported parameters. If the agent’s logic, prompts, control flow or guardrails are modified beyond those parameters, the certification stops applying to the modified version. That is not a penalty — it is simply that we can only stand behind what we built.

If the subscription ends

Certification status lapses and the version should no longer be described as a current Colleague AI Certified Release. Your licence to the agent package is unaffected, and so is your access to what you have already downloaded.
