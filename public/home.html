What it does

One register your team can trust, instead of a spreadsheet nobody updates.

Built for legal, procurement and finance teams on Microsoft 365 that track notice periods and renewals by hand today.

Terms extracted with evidence

Parties, dates, notice periods, renewals and amendments, each with the excerpt it was taken from, so a reviewer can check it in seconds.

A person confirms every entry

Nothing enters the register until a named reviewer approves it. The review itself is recorded, which is the evidence an auditor asks for.

Deadlines recalculated daily

Notice periods, including month-based ones, are recomputed every day and the effective terms after amendments are kept, so a renewal does not slip past.

Answers in Teams

Ask what a contract says or what ends this quarter. The agent answers from the reviewed register, not from a guess.
