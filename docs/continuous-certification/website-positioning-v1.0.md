## What you get

### Included with every licence, subscription or not
- The agent version you bought, perpetually, with its documentation and release-time evidence summary.
- Security fixes and defect corrections for the 24-month support period, at no further licence fee. ⚖
- Notice from us if a vulnerability or a change in the law affects your version, and the fix where the law requires it from us. ⚖
- No phone-home, no remote disabling: your installed agent keeps working whatever happens to our services.

### Added by Continuous Certification
- **A certified release that stays current.** Your version stays listed as a current Colleague AI Certified Release while you subscribe.
- **Changes assessed in the open.** For each relevant change in the covered scope, we publish whether it requires a new release and why, against your certificate.
- **Updated certified releases when needed.** When a covered change requires one, you receive the updated certified release at no further licence fee. You decide when to install it.
- **A certificate others can check.** A Certificate ID your auditors, customers or procurement team can verify online.
- **Regular evidence.** A standard evidence report and a consumption benchmark for your release. Shown as "planned" until it is a contractual commitment (spec §4.2).

### Always yours to run
Your deployment and your compliance stay in your hands.
- We give you a Deployment Assurance Kit: a checklist and a report template to confirm your installation matches the certified configuration.
- We never need access to your environment, and we don't take it.

## How it works (four steps)

1. **Built against one standard.** Every certified release is assessed against the same 86 controls in 18 areas, from human oversight and audit trails to model changes, data lineage and safe decommissioning.
2. **Checked by someone who did not build it.** ◆ A qualified reviewer, independent of the build, reviews the evidence for every certified release.
3. **Watched, weekly.** We track changes in the EU AI Act, our own standard, and breaking changes in Copilot Studio and the Power Platform that affect your release.
4. **Updated only when it matters.** No release for the sake of a release. If nothing in the covered scope changes, nothing is owed, and you can see why.

## What a certificate says, and what it does not ⚖

A Colleague AI Certificate states that a specific release of a Colleague AI agent package meets the Colleague AI Certified standard, and whether it is current.

It does **not**:
- certify your organisation, your deployment or your use;
- confirm that you comply with any law;
- serve as a regulator's, notified body's or accreditation body's assessment.

Colleague AI Certified is our own programme.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need the subscription to get security fixes?**
No. Security fixes and legally required corrections go to every licensee during the support period. ⚖

**What happens if I stop subscribing?**
- Your licence and your installed agent are unaffected.
- The certificate shows that the release was certified at release and is no longer current.

**Will you install updates for us?**
No. We publish the certified release and its notes. Your team installs it when it suits you, using the same documented process as the first installation. Hands-on help is available as a separately quoted service.

**Does this make our use of AI compliant?**
No programme can promise that. Compliance depends on your configuration, data, processes, sector and jurisdiction. We give you the evidence about our product, and a checklist for your deployment.

**How are suspension and revocation handled?** ⚖
- A certificate can be **suspended** for:
  - an unmitigated critical vulnerability;
  - a failed critical safety test;
  - misuse of the mark.
- It is **revoked** only for:
  - falsified evidence;
  - a defect that cannot be fixed.
- You hear from us first, except in an emergency, and you can appeal.

**What does it cost?**
The prices are those already published per tier (from $99 to $399 a month, or 10 months' price for a year). They are shown with "Coming soon" until sale is authorised.
