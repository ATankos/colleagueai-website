## Part B — Rights chain and corporate procedure (proposed answers for counsel)

1. **Exclusive licence, not assignment.** Czech copyright cannot be assigned during the author's life (§ 26 Act No.
   121/2000 Coll.); the instrument is an exclusive licence under §§ 2358 ff. Civil Code with the right to sublicense
   (§ 2363). Written form.
2. **No employee or commissioned work is assumed.** No employment or commissioning contract is on record for the period
   in which the works were created (May–November 2026, after the company was registered on 11 May 2026). If Alexandr had
   an employment with the company covering this work, § 58 may already give the company the economic rights to some
   works; A1 asks. Counsel decides how works created after signature are covered (§ 2 below, or a commission or
   employment arrangement).
3. **AI-assisted parts.** The licence covers the rights the licensor holds, including his human-authored selection,
   arrangement and original contributions; it does not claim copyright in machine-generated elements without human
   authorship.
4. **Chain to customers.** Author (→ holding, only if A1 applies) → Colleague AI s.r.o. → customer under the Software &
   Agent Licence. Each link must exist in writing before the customer licence states that the company owns or is duly
   licensed to sublicense the rights.
5. **Instrument A (Alexandr → company): conflict procedure.** Alexandr is, on the register, neither director nor
   shareholder, so §§ 54–57 ZOK are not triggered by his status alone. Counsel checks whether he is a "person close"
   to the director or otherwise connected (§ 54(1)) — if so, the director's notification under § 54 is made to the
   supreme body (the sole shareholder) and recorded. Otherwise no corporate decision is needed and **none is invented**.
6. **Instrument B (Marek → company), only if A2 lists works.** Marek would sign on both sides: as licensor and as the
   company's sole director. § 54 requires him to notify the conflict to the supreme body; as he is also the sole
   shareholder, the notification and any decision are recorded in writing as a sole-shareholder decision (§ 12 ZOK)
   before signature. Counsel confirms whether that is sufficient or whether the transaction needs another signatory
   (for example a second director appointed for the purpose, or a procurator).
7. **Evidence to keep (not published):** the signed instrument(s) with Schedule A (and B) and their SHA-256, any
   written record under 5 or 6, the accountant's note on consideration (A4), the author → holding agreement if A1
   applies, and the official extract used on the signing date.
