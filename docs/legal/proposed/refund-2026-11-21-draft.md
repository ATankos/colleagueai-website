## 1. Evaluate before you buy

Before you pay you can read the factsheet and product facts, book a guided evaluation on sample data in a test
tenant, use the ROI calculator with your own figures and read the governance evidence in the Trust Center. We ask every
customer to do that, because an Agent Package is digital content that cannot be returned once delivered.

## 2. When you get your money back

You are entitled to a refund in these cases, and this section is the rule, not the exception:

- **the package does not conform** to its factsheet and we cannot fix it (§5);
- **we charged you by mistake**, twice, or for an order we do not fulfil (§6);
- **a purchase was made by a consumer** and is cancelled under §4;
- **a subscription is affected** by a change we make (§7).

Outside those cases, a delivered Agent Package is not refunded merely because you no longer want it: the software,
its workflows and documentation cannot be given back in any meaningful way once downloaded. Mandatory law that gives
a business customer further remedies for defective performance is not limited by this policy.

## 3. Business customers

Our products are sold to businesses and other legal entities. Statutory consumer withdrawal rights do not apply to a
purchase made in the course of business.

## 4. Consumers

We do not sell to consumers, and checkout requires the buyer company's identification and a declaration that the
purchase is made in the course of its business; we rely on that declaration and do not detect consumer purchases
automatically. If a purchase is nevertheless made by a consumer, tell us within 14 days of the purchase: we cancel
the order, revoke the licence and refund the full price. We do not rely on a waiver of the withdrawal right for
digital content.

## 5. Defective or non-conforming packages

If a delivered package materially fails to conform to its published factsheet or installation guide, contact us within
30 days of delivery with your order number and a description (and, where possible, the readiness-check output). We
will first try to remedy the problem — a corrective release, a corrected package or corrected documentation — within
a reasonable time, normally 30 days (the corrective-release commitment of Licence §5(b) applies for its period). If we cannot deliver a conforming package within that time, you may choose a
proportionate price reduction or a full refund of the affected package, in which case the licence to it ends. These
remedies are in addition to any rights that the Czech Civil Code gives a business customer for defective performance.

A problem caused by your Microsoft environment, by a configuration outside the installation guide, by Microsoft
capacity that was not provided, or by modifications you made is not a non-conformity, but we will still help you find
the cause.

## 6. Duplicate or erroneous charges

Duplicate payments, charges made in error and charges for orders we do not fulfil are always refunded in full. Refunds
go to the original payment method, normally within 10 business days of our confirmation. A refund is documented by a
credit note that references the original invoice.

## 7. Subscriptions

Where we bill a service on a recurring basis (for example Continuous Certification, when offered), you can cancel at any
time with effect from the end of the current billing period; a started period is not refunded pro rata unless
mandatory law requires it. Two exceptions follow from the Continuous Certification terms: if we narrow the covered scope
of Continuous Certification you may end it and receive a pro-rata refund of the unused term; if we discontinue a
package, the subscription ends and the unused term is refunded pro rata. Your perpetual licence to the version already
purchased is unaffected in both cases.

## 8. How to ask

E-mail hello@colleagueai.ai from the address used at purchase, with the order number, the package name and the reason.
We confirm receipt within 5 business days and resolve the request within 30 days.

## 9. Chargebacks

If you dispute a charge with your bank or card issuer without contacting us first, we may suspend access to the
affected package while the dispute is examined. Please write to us first; most problems are solved faster that way.
