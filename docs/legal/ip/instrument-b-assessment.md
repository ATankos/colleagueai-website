## Legal points that decide the outcome

1. **Original authorship versus contribution.** Copyright protects a work as the author's unique creative result. Ideas,
   comments, direction, business requirements and feedback are not works. Review comments and instructions on
   Alexandr's drafts do not make Marek a co-author. Text he wrote himself and that was used substantially as written
   may be his work.
2. **Works created as managing director: § 58(10) Copyright Act.**
   - The employee-work rules (§ 58(1)–(6) and (8)) apply by analogy to works created to perform duties arising from a
     person's relationship to a legal entity of whose statutory body he is a member. The legal entity is then treated
     as the employer.
   - Works Marek created as jednatel to perform that function are therefore likely already exercised by the company
     without a licence.
   - Works he created outside that function, before the company existed (it was registered on 11 May 2026), or as a
     private person are not covered.
   - Counsel confirms which of his works, if any, fall under § 58(10).
3. **If Instrument B is needed**, Marek would sign on both sides: as licensor, and for the company as its sole
   jednatel, being also its sole shareholder.
   - **§ 13 Business Corporations Act** requires **written form with officially verified signatures** for a contract
     between a single-member company represented by its sole shareholder and that shareholder, unless the contract is
     concluded in the ordinary course of business on usual terms. An IP licence from the shareholder is unlikely to be
     ordinary-course.
   - **§§ 54–55 Business Corporations Act** (conflict of interest; contract with a member of the elected body). See
     `corporate-authority-checklist.md`; counsel decides the record to keep.
