# Remuneration options for Instrument A (clause 9): for founder and accountant decision

**Nothing here is chosen.** The founders choose one option. The accountant assesses its consequences before signature.
Remuneration is separate from equity: a share, a profit right or a founders' arrangement is not consideration for the
licence, and the instrument says so (Recital C, clause 9).

## Rules that apply to every option (Civil Code, verified 22 Nov 2026)

| Rule | Basis |
|---|---|
| A licence is for remuneration unless agreed otherwise; a gratuitous licence is valid when the parties agree it | § 2358(1), § 2366(1)(b) |
| Remuneration reflects the purpose, manner and scope of use and the author's contribution; a lump sum only in justified cases | § 2374(1) |
| If remuneration is manifestly disproportionate to the revenues from the licence, the author may claim fair additional remuneration; this right cannot be excluded or waived | § 2374(2) |
| For a paid licence, the licensee informs the author about the use at least once a year; cannot be excluded | § 2374a |
| Revenue-based remuneration: the author may inspect the records; statements at least yearly | § 2366(2)–(3) |

Amortised intangible asset or expense: accountant decides by amount and useful life | expense as revenue arises; yearly statement; accrual at year end | no cost recorded. Accountant to assess whether a gratuitous use of rights between these parties creates a taxable benefit or needs a valuation |
| Author: tax | income from licence fees (personal income tax). If the Author is a VAT payer or identified person, the licence may be a taxable supply; his own adviser decides | same, on each payment | none from the licence itself. Adviser confirms |
| Related-party pricing | if the parties are related for tax purposes, arm's-length price expected | arm's-length rate | accountant assesses whether non-arm's-length treatment has consequences |
| § 2374(2) risk (additional remuneration) | higher if the fee is small against later revenues | lower: grows with revenue | present: zero remuneration against future revenue |
| Cash for the company now | payment required | none until revenue | none |
