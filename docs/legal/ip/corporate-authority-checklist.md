# Corporate authority and conflicts: signing-day checklist

Complete on the day Instrument A (and, only if needed, Instrument B) is signed. Keep the completed checklist and the
documents privately. Record only their SHA-256 and a reference in the evidence manifest.

## Legal basis

Verified against the statutory text (BusinessCenter.cz) on 22 Nov 2026:
- § 12 ZOK (sole shareholder exercises the general meeting's powers);
- § 13 ZOK (contract with the sole shareholder);
- §§ 54–57 ZOK (conflict of interest; contracts with members of bodies and persons close to them; ordinary-course
  exception).

**Do not assume.** Item 6 decides whether anything corporate is required for Instrument A. Being both director and sole
shareholder does not by itself require Marek to notify himself. The statute ties the duties to the specific contract
and to the persons involved.
