## Recitals

(A) The Author created the works listed in Schedule A. The Author represents that, when he created them, he was neither
the Company's employee nor a member of its bodies, and did not create them under a contract for work or another
commission from the Company or from anyone else. The Author declares these facts in clause 10.1.

(B) Under Czech law, the author's economic rights cannot be transferred. The Company therefore needs a licence to
exploit the works, to grant sublicences to its customers and partners, and to develop its products.

(C) This Agreement concerns rights in works only. It does not concern, and does not imply, any ownership interest in
the Company, any share, any right to profit, any employment or any other relationship between the parties.

## 1. Definitions

1.1 "**Works**" means the works listed in Schedule A as identified there at the cut-off commit, together with the
works added by an Accession Schedule under clause 5, in every version up to the identification stated in the relevant
schedule.

1.2 "**Accession Schedule**" means a written schedule in the form of Schedule B, signed by both parties, that adds
specifically identified works to this Agreement.

1.3 "**Products**" means the Company's software, Agent Packages (managed Microsoft Power Platform solutions and their
documentation), website, services and other offerings, now or in the future, in which a Work is used.

1.4 "**Customer**" means anyone who acquires from the Company, or from a Partner, a right to use a Product.

1.5 "**Partner**" means an implementation partner, reseller, distributor or other intermediary of the Company acting
under a written agreement with the Company.

1.6 "**Generic Know-how**" means general skills, knowledge, experience, methods, techniques, ideas and concepts that
are not themselves protected by copyright, including programming knowledge and knowledge of Microsoft platforms, as
well as publicly available information.

1.7 "**Field**" means the Company's business as described in Schedule A, part 1.

## 2. Grant of licence

2.1 The Author grants the Company an **exclusive licence** to exercise the right to use the Works, in original form,
modified form, or combined with other works.

2.2 The licence is granted:
- (a) for **all methods of use known at the time this Agreement is concluded** (§ 2372(1) of the Civil Code);
- (b) **territorially unlimited** (worldwide);
- (c) **for the entire term of the economic rights** to the Works;
- (d) **quantitatively unlimited**.

The parties expressly agree that the statutory presumptions limiting the scope of a licence (§ 2376(2) of the Civil
Code: territory of the Czech Republic, customary period of no more than one year, customary quantity) do not apply.

2.3 For clarity, the licence includes in particular:
- reproduction, permanent and temporary, by any means;
- distribution of copies;
- communication to the public, including making available online and in customers' tenants;
- modification, translation, adaptation, arrangement and other alteration;
- combination with other works and inclusion in collective works and databases;
- the creation of derivative works.

For computer programs it further includes use in source and object code, compilation, decompilation within the limits
of the law, porting, integration, and the creation of new versions and releases.

2.4 Exclusivity (§ 2360 of the Civil Code): while this Agreement is in force, the Author shall not grant a licence to the
Works to any third party, and shall not use the Works himself in a way covered by the licence. Clause 16 applies.

2.5 The Company is **not obliged to exercise the licence** (§ 2372(2) of the Civil Code: the parties agree otherwise).
This does not affect the Author's mandatory rights under clause 12.2.

2.6 This Agreement does not transfer copyright. The Author keeps his moral rights (clause 7) and the economic rights,
whose exercise is licensed by this Agreement.

## 3. Sublicences and assignment of the licence

3.1 The Company may grant sublicences to the Works, in whole or in part, exclusive or non-exclusive, gratuitous or for
consideration, without further consent of the Author (§ 2363 of the Civil Code), in particular to:
- (a) Customers, including the right to install the Products in the Customer's own environment or tenant (for example
  a Microsoft 365 / Power Platform tenant), to use and back them up and to modify them as the Customer's terms permit;
- (b) Partners, for the purpose of marketing, reselling, implementing, integrating and supporting the Products,
  including the right to grant sub-sublicences to Customers;
- (c) contractors and service providers of the Company, for the purpose of developing, operating, testing, auditing
  and supporting the Products.

3.2 The Author gives his **written consent in advance** (§ 2364 of the Civil Code) to the Company assigning the licence,
in whole or in part, to:
- (a) a person that acquires the Company's business or the part of it to which the Works relate;
- (b) a person resulting from a merger, division or other transformation of the Company;
- (c) a person controlled by the same persons as the Company, or controlling the Company, within a group restructuring.

The Company shall inform the Author of the assignment without undue delay and name the assignee. Succession under
§ 2383 of the Civil Code is not excluded.

## 4. Installation in customer environments

4.1 The parties acknowledge that the Products are delivered as packages that the Customer installs and operates in its
own environment, and that the Company has no access to the Customer's data. A Customer's installation, configuration,
operation and permitted modification under its licence terms are uses covered by clause 3.1(a).

## 5. Future works: Accession Schedules

5.1 This Agreement does **not** cover works the Author may create in the future, unless they are added by an Accession
Schedule. No general or blanket licence to all future works of the Author is granted.

5.2 An Accession Schedule identifies the added works objectively, in particular by:
- the repository and commit (cut-off);
- the paths and their tree hashes;
- the file names, versions and SHA-256 fingerprints of released packages;
- or another unambiguous identifier.

The licence to the added works arises on the date both parties sign the Accession Schedule, on the terms of this
Agreement. The parties intend to sign an Accession Schedule at least on every new release of a Product and at least once
per calendar quarter in which the Author contributed works in the Field.

5.3 Modifications that the Company, a Customer or a Partner makes to a Work under the licence are not works of the
Author, and require no Accession Schedule.

## 6. Modifications and derivative works

6.1 The Author consents (§ 2375 of the Civil Code) to the Company, and to persons authorised by it under clause 3, making
alterations to the Works and their titles, combining them with other works, including them in collective works, and
creating derivative works. The Author does not reserve his consent.

6.2 The rights in modifications and derivative works created by the Company or by third parties for the Company are
governed by the relationships under which they were created, and are not subject to remuneration under clause 9.

## 7. Moral rights

7.1 The Author's moral rights are not affected. The Author agrees that the Works may be published and used without
naming the Author, under the Company's name or a product name, and that his designation may be altered or omitted.

7.2 The Author keeps the right to object to a use of the Works that would distort them or otherwise derogate from their
value, or that would prejudice his honour or reputation.

8.2 The licence applies to all rights in the Works that exist, in particular the Author's human creative contribution,

contribution may not be protected by copyright**. This Agreement does not claim such protection, and the Author gives no
warranty that it exists. The Company may use such outputs to the extent the law and the terms of the relevant tool permit.

8.3 Each party shall inform the other without undue delay if it learns that the terms of an AI tool restrict the use of
outputs in a way relevant to the Products.
