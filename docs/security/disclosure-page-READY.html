Security

Reporting a security vulnerability

If you believe you have found a security vulnerability in a ColleagueAI product (for example the Contract Intelligence & Register package) or in colleagueai.ai, tell us at security@colleagueai.ai. Include the product and release number (from your package or order page), what you observed, how to reproduce it and how we can reach you. You receive an automatic acknowledgement with a reference number, and a person answers within one business day.

What we ask

  • Do not access, change or delete data that is not yours; do not test against customer environments or live payment flows.
  • No social engineering, denial of service or physical attacks. Test only against your own installation or a trial environment.
  • Give us a reasonable time to fix the problem before you publish; we aim for coordinated disclosure within 90 days of confirmation.

What we do

  • We confirm the report and tell you the result of our triage within two business days.
  • We fix confirmed vulnerabilities and release a corrective version through our normal release process: a new release number and a new published SHA-256 checksum, never a silent replacement.
  • We notify the licensees of affected releases by e-mail to the purchasing contact on their order.
  • Where the EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847) requires it, we report actively exploited vulnerabilities and severe incidents to the authorities within the statutory deadlines.
  • We credit reporters who wish to be named, after the fix is available. We do not currently pay bounties.

Scope

ColleagueAI products and colleagueai.ai. Microsoft Power Platform, Copilot Studio, Dataverse, Stripe, Vercel and other providers run their own programmes; please report issues in those platforms to them.

Supported releases

Security fixes for a purchased release are provided as corrective releases at no further licence fee during the support window stated in the Software & Agent Licence [D1: 24 months from delivery]. The current release of each product and the status of earlier releases are listed on the product's factsheet.
